    Questions people ask

    GatherGuide can explain any of these in more detail.

    How do I reach a person on your team?

    Email dhivyav@usf.edu. Include your event brief from GatherGuide or the estimate tool so we can start your quote.

    Does my inquiry reserve the date?

    No. Only a signed agreement and the deposit reserve a date. GatherGuide can’t see our calendar, so it can’t confirm availability.

    How much is the deposit?

    30% of the approved written quote, due when you sign the agreement. The balance is due 7 days before the event.

    When do you need the final guest count?

    7 days before the event. Lowering the count after that doesn’t lower the agreed food total. Raising it needs written approval.

    What is your cancellation policy?

    15 or more days out, we keep the deposit and refund other payments. 8 to 14 days out, the charge is 50% of the quote. Within 7 days, it’s 100%. Anything you’ve already paid counts toward the charge.

    How far ahead should I order?

    3 days for Office Lunch and Breakfast, 7 days for the Celebration Buffet and 21 days for the Signature Reception. Shorter notice goes to our team for review.

    Is buffet setup the same as having staff?

    No. Buffet setup means we arrange the food and serving utensils. Attendants who serve guests are a separate add-on at $35 per staff-hour.

    Can you make a menu nut-free or allergy-safe?

    We can’t guarantee it. Shared preparation can cause cross-contact, so a person on our team reviews every allergy request.

    Do you have halal or kosher certification?

    No. We don’t hold or claim any religious or gluten-free certification.

    Do you serve alcohol?

    No. Alcohol and bartending are outside our services. We offer lemonade and iced tea as a drink add-on.

    Is tax included in estimates?

    No. Estimates are before tax, and the written quote shows the final amount.
